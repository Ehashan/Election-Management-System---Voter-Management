Voter Approval Guide for Registration Officers

Standard operating procedure for reviewing and verifying new voter submissions:

Step 1

Access Admin Portal

Sign in to the administrative console using your official staff username and password.

Step 2

Review Pending Signups

Navigate to 'Manage Signups' to inspect newly submitted citizen registrations and SLIN/NIC entries.

Step 3

Verify Identity & Facial Photo

Cross-check the Grama Niladari division and inspect the live webcam face capture for clarity and lighting.

Step 4

Approve & Issue Voter ID

Generate the district-coded Voter ID and mark the registration as 'Verified' in the official registry.
