What You Need to Register

Please have the following items ready before starting your registration:

Valid NIC / SLIN

Sri Lankan National Identity Card (Old 9-digit+V or New 12-digit format).

Active Mobile Number

A working Sri Lankan mobile number to receive the 6-digit SMS verification code.

Working Camera

Webcam or mobile device camera with adequate lighting for live facial capture.

Address & GN Division

Your current residential street, postal code, and Grama Niladari (GN) division.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding registration, verification, and privacy.

Why is my face captured during registration? ▾

Your live face photo is captured to prevent voter impersonation and ensure that only you can cast your vote. On election day, your identity is verified at the polling booth using biometric facial verification against this secure record.

How long does approval take? ▾

Voter registrations are reviewed and verified by district election officials typically within 1 to 3 business days after address and GN division verification.

What if my photo is rejected? ▾

If your photograph is blurry, obstructed, or lacks proper lighting, your profile will remain in 'Pending' status. You can simply sign in to your voter account and capture a clearer photo using the retake tool.

Who is eligible to register as a voter in Sri Lanka? ▾

Any citizen of Sri Lanka who is 18 years of age or older on the qualifying date, of sound mind, and not disqualified by law is legally eligible to register.

Can I update my address or polling division later? ▾

Yes. You can sign in and update your residential address, administrative district, and Grama Niladari division whenever you relocate before the annual electoral register is certified.

Is my personal information and biometric data secure? ▾

Yes, absolutely. All sensitive identity records, personal information, and biometric face captures are encrypted in transit and at rest on state election servers in accordance with strict national data protection regulations.
