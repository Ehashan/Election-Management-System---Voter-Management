Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions about registration checking, approval timelines, and data privacy.

How do I know if my voter registration was approved? ▼

Use the "Check Registration Status" tool above by entering your National Identity Card (NIC) number. If approved, your status will display "Approved", and you are fully eligible to vote at your assigned polling booth.

What does "Pending approval" mean? ▼

"Pending approval" means your registration submission has been safely received and is undergoing review by Election Registration Officers to verify residential boundaries and photo quality. Approval typically takes 1 to 3 business days.

Can anyone else see my personal information using the status check? ▼

No. The status check is privacy-hardened. It returns ONLY the verification status ("Approved", "Pending approval", or "Not found") and the registration date. It never reveals names, addresses, photographs, or contact numbers. It is also limited to 5 checks per minute to prevent automated guessing.

What should I do if my status displays "Not found"? ▼

If your status is "Not found", you may not have completed your voter registration. Please navigate to the "For Citizens" tab to create your account and complete detailed registration with your webcam facial photo.

How can I update my address or polling division? ▼

You can sign in to your voter account at any time before the annual electoral register certification deadline and update your residential address, administrative district, and Grama Niladari division.

Privacy Policy & Biometric Data Notice

Clear and transparent explanation of what voter data is collected, including face images, and why.

1. What Data We Collect

When you register as an elector in the Election Management System, we collect the following essential categories of information:

  • Identity & Demographic Information: National Identity Card (SLIN / NIC) number, full name, date of birth, gender, and mobile telephone number.
  • Residential & Electoral Mapping: Administrative District, Electoral District, Polling Division, Postal Code, Grama Niladari (GN) Division, street address, and household number.
  • Biometric Facial Imagery: High-resolution live webcam photograph of your face captured directly during the final step of voter registration.

2. Why We Collect This Data & How It Is Used

Legal Eligibility Mandate

To verify that the elector meets constitutional voting age criteria (18 years or older on qualifying date) and is ordinarily resident in the designated electoral division.

Impersonation & Fraud Prevention

Facial biometric images are captured to uniquely authenticate electors at polling booths on election day. Live camera comparison completely eliminates double-voting and voter impersonation fraud.

Encrypted Biometric Storage

Face photos and derived mathematical feature embeddings are encrypted at rest using AES-256 cryptographic standards. Raw unencrypted biometric streams are never transmitted over public networks.

PDPA No. 9 of 2022 Compliance

All records are processed strictly under statutory public-interest provisions of the Sri Lankan Personal Data Protection Act. Citizen data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for commercial purposes.
